# NUHAS HSE360 V8.10.5 — Permit, location and quick-report workflows

## Implemented in this increment

- Added a Locations register with name, code, parent area, location type, known hazards/access notes and active/inactive selection.
- Added location autocomplete suggestions to HSE record forms. Users can choose a saved location or type a new one immediately; duplicate saved location names are blocked.
- Reworked observation, near-miss and first-aid forms toward shorter structured entries with task/event categories, immediate actions, potential severity and referral details where relevant.
- Added a three-class permit classification guide: General Work, Hot Work (welding/cutting/grinding) and Critical Work (confined space, height, electrical, lifting, excavation, line breaking, chemical work and energy isolation/LOTO).
- Added receiver department/head, HSE reviewer, approval stage and separate issuer/receiver/HSE name-and-time sign-off fields.
- Added sign-off buttons that insert the currently authenticated profile display name and local timestamp into the selected role's fields.
- Permit authorisation now requires all three sign-offs and distinct signer names in the frontend, in addition to existing permit safety-control checks.
- JSA picker now labels templates by permit class and filters standard templates by the selected permit class. Added routine cleaning/housekeeping, lubrication/PPM, visual inspection and routine material transfer starters.
- Existing records and features remain in the cumulative source. No Supabase SQL/config/storage changes or production writes were made.

## Important limitations

- This increment does not claim to be an official ADNOC permit form. It uses a concise industry-style PTW layout and requires NUHAS to review/approve its controlled form and procedure against applicable ADNOC/client requirements, ISO 45001, UAE legal obligations and the company's ADOSH-SF obligations before operational adoption.
- Typed name + timestamp is an acknowledgement record, not a cryptographic signature. The current frontend sign buttons are not sufficient identity/role enforcement. Profile-bound issuer/receiver/HSE approvals, department-head routing, notifications, immutable signature audit trail and server-side sequential approval must be completed and tested during the final backend integration phase.
- The current frontend's status permissions remain in force; this is not a replacement for server-side permit authorisation enforcement.
- Location register persistence depends on the existing generic HSE record service accepting the `locations` module. It has not been tested against live Supabase. If module permissions or a backend module allowlist rejects it, do not deploy until reconciled.
- JSA items are starter templates and must be reviewed against actual job conditions; they are not approved task-specific risk assessments.
- No GitHub push, deployment or database migration was performed.
