# NUHAS HSE360 V8.8.6 — Certificate importer quality and OCR safeguards

## Why this update was made

The V8.8.5 reader could flatten selectable PDF text into a single line. Label/value matching could then associate unrelated fragments. It could also accept symbol-heavy OCR output (for example, `7 >< —`) as an issuer, and its fallback title did not distinguish a filename suggestion from a title found in the document.

## Implemented in the local cumulative master

- **Preserves selectable-PDF reading order:** PDF text items are grouped by vertical baseline and sorted by horizontal position, instead of indiscriminately joining every text item with spaces.
- **Uses quality-based OCR fallback:** short or low-quality selectable text triggers browser-side OCR. The app compares a simple local text-quality score before replacing existing selectable text, and retains the original text when OCR does not score better.
- **Improved scan OCR:** renders pages at 2.0 scale, uses Tesseract sparse-text segmentation for certificate layouts, and retries low-confidence documents of up to three pages with an alternate block-text layout. The higher-scoring output is retained.
- **Surfaces OCR confidence:** average Tesseract confidence is displayed. Low and moderate confidence explicitly prompt manual review.
- **Rejects implausible field suggestions:** issuer suggestions must contain plausible alphabetic organization text. Symbol-heavy and word-fragment OCR output is left blank and flagged for manual entry.
- **Improves title suggestions:** common inspection/calibration/conformity/permit document titles are normalized. Award-style documents with `Presented to`/`Awarded to` wording or `HSE HERO` filenames receive a reviewable `Certificate of Appreciation / Recognition` suggestion. Filename fallbacks are explicitly identified as filename-derived.
- **Adds Certificate holder / recipient:** a distinct editable field recognizes common `Presented to`, `Awarded to`, `Issued to`, holder and recipient labels. The reviewed value is stored in the certificate record separately from the renewal-responsible person. This uses the existing record JSON and requires no new table migration.
- **Improves date recognition:** supports ordinal day suffixes, common month-name formats with hyphens, and additional labels such as `Valid till`, `Valid up to`, and `Date of expiration`. Ambiguous numeric dates default to DD/MM/YYYY for UAE use; every extracted date still requires human confirmation.
- **Keeps human review mandatory:** a missed or uncertain number, issuer, issue/expiry date or recipient remains blank for manual review. OCR does not establish authenticity or compliance.

## Not verified / limitations

- The exact PDF shown in the user's screenshot was not provided as a source PDF, so OCR cannot be measured against that file here. Browser-side OCR may still fail on low-resolution, decorative, skewed, handwritten, or multilingual documents.
- No full authenticated browser workflow or live Supabase upload/download test was executed.
- Candidate SQL migrations have not been executed against PostgreSQL/Supabase. No GitHub push or production database change was made.
